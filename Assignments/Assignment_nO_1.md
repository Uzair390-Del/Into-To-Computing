## 1. Assignment Title

**Developing a Professional Business Report and Presentation Using MS Word and MS PowerPoint**

## 2. Assignment Objectives

The purpose of this assignment is to develop students' basic computer skills and introduce them to professional business documentation and presentations. Students will learn to use MS Word and MS PowerPoint to prepare business-related documents and communicate business ideas effectively.

Upon completion, students should be able to:
- Create and format professional documents using MS Word.
- Apply headings, page layouts, tables, images, headers, footers, and page numbers.
- Design a professional presentation using MS PowerPoint.
- Organize business information in a clear and structured manner.
- Apply basic computing skills to common BBA-related tasks.

## 3. Assignment Scenario

Imagine that you are a junior business executive working for a small company. Your manager has asked you to prepare a short business report and a presentation introducing a business of your choice.

You may select a business such as:
- A clothing brand
- A restaurant or café
- An online shopping store
- A mobile accessories business
- A supermarket
- A cosmetics brand
- A startup of your own choice

Your task is to prepare a professional business report in MS Word and a corresponding presentation in MS PowerPoint.

## 4. Part A: MS Word — Business Report

Prepare a business report of approximately **4–6 pages** using MS Word.

Your report must contain the following sections:

**1. Cover Page**

Include the business name, assignment title, student name, roll number, program, semester, subject, instructor name, and submission date.

**2. Introduction to the Business**

- Name and type of business.
- Products or services offered.
- Brief introduction and purpose of the business.

**3. Business Objectives**

Write at least three objectives of the selected business.

**4. Target Customers**

Identify the intended customers of the business based on factors such as age, income, interests, or location.

**5. Marketing Strategy**

Briefly explain how the business will promote its products or services. You may discuss social media, advertising, discounts, or other marketing methods.

**6. Basic Financial Information**

Create a table showing estimated startup costs for at least five items, such as equipment, inventory, rent, marketing, and utilities. Include quantities, estimated costs, and a total.

**7. Conclusion**

Summarize the business idea and explain why it may be successful.

**8. References**

Mention any websites, books, or other sources used.

### MS Word Formatting Requirements

Your report must demonstrate the following skills:

- Use an appropriate font, such as Times New Roman or Arial, with consistent formatting.
- Use a suitable font size: 12 pt for body text and larger sizes for headings.
- Apply bold formatting and appropriate heading styles.
- Set page margins and use proper paragraph alignment and spacing.
- Insert at least one relevant business image or logo.
- Create at least one properly formatted table.
- Add page numbers and a header or footer.
- Use bullets or numbered lists where appropriate.
- Proofread the document for spelling and grammar.
- Maintain a clean, professional layout.

## 5. Part B: MS PowerPoint — Business Presentation

Create a PowerPoint presentation of **6–8 slides** based on your business report.

The presentation should include:

1. **Title Slide:** Business name, student name, roll number, and course.
2. **Business Introduction:** Overview of the business and its products or services.
3. **Business Objectives:** Main goals of the business.
4. **Target Customers:** Intended customer group.
5. **Marketing Strategy:** How the business will attract customers.
6. **Estimated Startup Costs:** A simple table or chart summarizing the costs.
7. **Competitive Advantage:** What makes the business different from competitors.
8. **Conclusion:** Summary of the business idea.

### MS PowerPoint Formatting Requirements

- Use a consistent and professional theme.
- Include appropriate titles and concise bullet points.
- Add relevant images, icons, or illustrations.
- Use readable font sizes and suitable color combinations.
- Insert at least one table, chart, or other visual representation of information.
- Apply slide transitions appropriately.
- Avoid excessive text, animations, and decorative effects.
- Ensure the slides are properly aligned and easy to understand.

## 6. Printed Submission Requirements

Students must submit a **printed hard copy** of the completed assignment.

The printed submission must contain:

1. The cover page.
2. The complete MS Word business report.
3. The MS PowerPoint presentation printed in handout format, preferably 2 or 4 slides per page.

Students should arrange the pages in the correct order and staple or bind them neatly. The report and presentation must be readable, properly aligned, and professionally formatted.

Students are encouraged to retain the original Word and PowerPoint files for their own records and for verification if requested by the instructor.

## 7. General Instructions

- This is an individual assignment.
- Every student must select a business idea and prepare their own report and presentation.
- Use realistic estimates for financial information and clearly label them as estimates.
- All information should be relevant to the selected business.
- Copying another student's work is not permitted.
- Use of templates is allowed, but students must demonstrate their own formatting and presentation skills.
- The assignment must be submitted in printed form by the announced deadline.
- Students should be prepared to explain their selected business and demonstrate the computer skills used in preparing the assignment.
